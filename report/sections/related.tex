\section{Related Work and Design Decisions}\label{sec:related}
\textbf{Autoencoders and losses.} Denoising autoencoders learn robust representations by reconstructing a clean input from a corrupted one~\cite{vincent2008}. The assignment forbids unrestricted skip connections, so Task~1 keeps the classical bottleneck; the price, measured in Section~\ref{sec:t1}, is that even clean images are reconstructed imperfectly. L1 gives sharper results than L2 but ignores structure, whereas SSIM~\cite{wang2004ssim} compares local luminance, contrast and structure; their combination trains restoration networks better than either alone~\cite{zhao2017loss}, so we use $\alpha\mathcal{L}_1+(1-\alpha)(1-\mathrm{SSIM})$ and let Optuna choose $\alpha$. SSIM reacts weakly to global colour shifts, which matters for Section~\ref{sec:t2}.

\textbf{Mixture of experts.} Gated combinations of specialised networks trained jointly were introduced in~\cite{jacobs1991}; sparsely-gated layers scaled the idea~\cite{shazeer2017}. Task~3 uses a dense softmax gate with a temperature and the squared-deviation balance loss given in the assignment, and starts from trained components instead of random ones.

\textbf{Conditional image translation.} Conditional GANs~\cite{mirza2014} were applied to paired translation by pix2pix~\cite{isola2017}, which combines a U-Net generator~\cite{ronneberger2015}, a PatchGAN discriminator and an L1 term with weight 100. FS2K~\cite{fan2022fs2k} provides 2,104 photo--sketch pairs in three styles. Pixel losses favour smooth outputs because perception and distortion trade off~\cite{blau2018}, which explains the softness of our sketches. Tuning uses Optuna~\cite{akiba2019} (TPE sampler, median pruning), tracking uses MLflow~\cite{mlflow}.

\textbf{Main decisions.} (i)~No skip connections in Tasks~1--3, as required; identity bypasses in Tasks~2 and~3 remove the resulting cost on clean images. (ii)~The L1/SSIM weight is searched by Optuna; when the first search chose an SSIM-heavy value that discarded colour, the range was restricted and the models retrained (Section~\ref{sec:t2}). (iii)~Hard routing and soft mixing are compared on identical inputs. (iv)~Non-square photographs for sketches are cropped by default, because stretching distorted faces in a manual test.
